Changelog / v0.0.0

2026-10-24v0.0.0

/Users/soumyachakraborty/Documents/VERCODE/website/content/changelog/2026-10-24.md

Added

  • The `website` static export: 30 routes — the marketing pages (`/`, `/features`, `/pricing`, `/faq`, `/about`), the 9-topic docs hub, the 2-entry changelog feed, the 2-post blog, and the 5 legal pages (`docs/WEBSITE/INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PAGES.md` §1's `WFR-101` route set).
  • The 16-question FAQ (`docs/WEBSITE/CONTENT_SCHEMAS.md §4`): the visible answers and the `FAQPage` JSON-LD both render from one source (`src/lib/faq.ts`), byte-equal by construction (the `docs/WEBSITE/SEO_AEO_GEO.md §3` consistency rule).
  • The 4-plan pricing page + the quota table, generated from `content/pricing/pricing.json` (the `docs/WEBSITE/INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PAGES.md §3.3` "a drift is structurally impossible" rule — the numbers are generated, not typed).
  • The machine-readable layer: the `sitemap.xml` + `robots.txt` + `llms.txt` + `llms-full.txt` generators (`website/src/lib/llms.ts`, the `docs/WEBSITE/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md §1` build-time emission) and the single-source JSON-LD generator (`website/src/lib/schema-generator.ts`, the `docs/WEBSITE/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md §4.1` "the only JSON-LD emitter").
  • The anti-slop lint gate: the `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §9/§11` tells (the gradient, the icon-topped card, the vanity totals) checked over the marketing surface as a 0-FAIL build gate.

Changed

  • The `Makefile` gained the `check-website` target (gate 14, the `docs/WEBSITE/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md §5` "a new website-specific lane"): the `@vercode/website` build + typecheck + the `UT-web-*` unit suite + the anti-slop lint, the website's verified-state gate.
  • The `.env.example` + the `packages/config` `KNOWN_VARS` gained the 3 website vars (`VERCODE_WEBSITE_BASE_URL`, `VERCODE_WEBSITE_OG_DEFAULT`, `VERCODE_BEACON_SECRET`; the `docs/OPERATIONS/ENVIRONMENT_VARIABLES.md §8.5` section).
