# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the VERCODE
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience,
gender identity and expression, age, body size, disability, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Behaviors that promote a healthy community

- Respect for people and their work, including in code review
- Embracing diverse perspectives and viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

### Behaviors that are unacceptable

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic
  address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

### Review-specific conduct

VERCODE is about AI-assisted code review, and reviews are public. A few extra
norms keep that safe:

- Critique the code and the design decision, never the person who wrote it.
- A finding without evidence (file, line, category, severity, confidence) is a
  guess, not a review — hold each other's reviews to that bar.
- Respect maintainers who close, reject, or defer a reported issue with a
  reasoned explanation.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and may take appropriate and fair action in response to any behavior
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, including:

- Issuing warnings for unacceptable behavior
- Removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues,
  and other contributions that are not suited to this Code of Conduct
- Banning, temporarily or permanently, any contributor for other behaviors that
  they deem inappropriate or threatening

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces
whenever an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples of
representing a project include using an official project e-mail address, posting
via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative
at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Reported incidents are handled as follows:

1. Any member of the project team (including maintainers and triagers) may be
   contacted to report an incident, via the project maintainers through the
   [security contact in SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by email to the repository
   owner.
2. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
3. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the
   reporter of an incident.
4. The project team will not disclose the reporter's identity without
   explicit permission.
5. The project team may choose to post a public summary of the final
   outcome for community transparency.

## Enforcement guidelines

The project team will act to make reasonable efforts to ensure comfort for
all participants. A violation of this Code of Conduct may result in one or
more of the following:

- **Warning** — a private written warning from the project team, with a clear
  statement of the behavior that violated this Code of Conduct, an
  explanation of why that behavior violated it, and the expected correction.
- **Reprimand** — a public warning by the project team, with a requirement
  that the person cease the behavior in question for 30 days and no contact
  with the project team during that period.
- **Ban** — a temporary or permanent ban from participation in the project.
