<p>VERCODE prices the capability, not the seats: a plan is a review capacity (reviews per month, a token-spend cap, a model tier), billed on the reviews a team runs, not on the heads that open pull requests. The 4-plan ladder is Free, Developer, Team, Enterprise.</p>
<p>The pricing figures on this page match <code class="mono">content/pricing/pricing.json</code> — the single source that feeds the /pricing page, the Product/Offer JSON-LD, and the /api/answer route in one build.</p>
<h2 id="the-ladder">The ladder</h2>
<div class="table-wrap"><table><caption>Plan, Price (per month), Role</caption><thead><tr><th scope="col">Plan</th><th scope="col">Price (per month)</th><th scope="col">Role</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Free</td><td>$0</td><td>The activation tier: a permanent Free org, not a trial</td></tr>
<tr><td>Developer</td><td>$24</td><td>An individual's first paid step: 10× the Free volume at the same per-review cost</td></tr>
<tr><td>Team</td><td>$199</td><td>The core commercial tier: the strong-model review pass, 5,000 reviews a month</td></tr>
<tr><td>Enterprise</td><td>Custom (negotiated)</td><td>BYOK and Ollama cost-shifting, frontier-model access, SSO/SCIM, data residency</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>The Free plan is a real product, not a time-boxed trial: a review is published, a finding is actionable, and the plan does not expire. That is the activation funnel — an org that activates on Free converts on the paid plans.</p>
<h2 id="what-each-step-buys">What each step buys</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Free → Developer</strong> is a volume step: 50 reviews a month becomes 500, at the same per-review cost and the same standard model tier.</li>
  <li><strong>Developer → Team</strong> is a capability step: 5,000 reviews a month, plus the strong-model access the deep and security passes use.</li>
  <li><strong>Team → Enterprise</strong> is a cost-shifting and compliance step: configurable caps, the frontier tier, BYOK and Ollama (a near-zero COGS account), and data residency.</li>
</ul>
<p>The per-plan quota detail — the exact review, file, concurrency, and retention numbers — is on the <a href="/docs/quotas/">quotas page</a>. The plan cards, the full quota comparison table, and the pricing FAQ are on the <a href="/pricing/">pricing page</a>.</p>
