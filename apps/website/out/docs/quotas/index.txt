<p>A plan's quota is a set of commercial limits, each a number, a unit, and a ceiling. The source of truth is <code class="mono">packages/contracts/src/plans.ts</code>; the API quota check, the worker pre-flight check, and the dashboard display all read from it. The table below is those values.</p>
<p>The caps are the plan's shape; the skip behavior at the cap is the NFR-18 rule: a stoppage is never silent.</p>

<p>Enterprise caps are configurable: the values shown are the floor, and a per-account negotiation raises them. A hardcoded &quot;unlimited&quot; is deliberately not a value: it would be a cost-abuse vector (a platform cap is the backstop).</p>
<h2 id="the-ceiling-semantics">The ceiling semantics</h2>
<p>Each dimension behaves differently at its cap:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Reviews per month</strong> is a hard cap: the N+1th review in a month skips with a notice (NFR-18). The 50th review on a 50-cap plan runs; the 51st skips. The skip is before the AI stage, so it consumes zero tokens.</li>
  <li><strong>Files and lines per review</strong> is a degrade, not a skip: a diff over the cap is reviewed on its highest-risk files, and the rest is listed in a summary appendix (NFR-19). An over-budget review does not error.</li>
  <li><strong>Concurrent reviews</strong> is a queue: a job over the in-flight cap waits, it is not skipped. A throughput cap delays; a volume cap skips.</li>
  <li><strong>Finding retention</strong> is a hard delete on schedule: findings expire on the plan's window (30 / 90 / 365 / custom days) and are purged on a retention job, not soft-flagged.</li>
  <li><strong>Custom rules</strong> is a count cap: a create over the cap is a 409 and a dashboard notice.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-80-warning">The 80% warning</h2>
<p>The dashboard's quota strip warns at 80% of a cap — a banner plus a dashboard row, the USER_JOURNEYS J4 early signal. An 80% near-miss is a warning; a 100% breach is a skip with a notice; a volume spike over 3× an org's 30-day baseline is an anomaly alert. Every one of those is a visible record, not a silent stop.</p>
