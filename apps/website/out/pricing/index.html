Pricing questions

Which plan should I start on?
Start on Free: it is a permanent plan, not a trial, with 50 reviews a month. Move to Developer when you need more volume at the same per-review cost; move to Team when the org needs the strong-model pass and 5,000 reviews a month. Enterprise is the cost-shifting and compliance shape: BYOK, Ollama, frontier access, SSO.
What happens when I reach the quota cap?
The review is skipped before the AI stage runs, so it consumes zero tokens, and a notice is posted on the pull request (NFR-18: skip-with-notice, never a silent stop). The dashboard shows the skip row, the reason, and the upgrade path; the 80% warning banner fires before the cap is reached.
Can I use my own provider key (BYOK)?
Yes, on the plans that carry provider settings. The key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a KMS-wrapped master key (FR-313), masked in the dashboard, and revocable at any time. With BYOK or a self-hosted Ollama, the model spend is on your bill, not VERCODE's.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes: a Team or Enterprise annual prepay carries a discount for the commit. The exact discount percentage is a market decision and is set per plan, not a fixed published figure. Annual billing reduces churn and improves the NRR the launch target names.
What if I exceed my quota during the month?
The over-cap review is skipped with a notice, not silently billed beyond the cap (NFR-18). On Team and Enterprise, a metered overage is available as an opt-in, at a published per-review rate with a cap; Free and Developer are hard caps. The overage is transparent: the dashboard shows what will be billed before it bills.
Can I self-host VERCODE?
The code is MIT-licensed and self-hostable. The hosted pipeline requires a GitHub App installation and an AI provider key. With BYOK or Ollama the model spend is on your bill, not VERCODE's — the Enterprise plan is the commercial shape built around that cost-shifting.
