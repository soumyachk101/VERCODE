Status

Last updated 2026-10-23 · Contact security@vercode.com

The incident record is maintained at the external status service — this page is the link, not the record. Each entry is a single incident: a window (start to resolution), a detection time, and a fix. No color-coded theater, no uptime percentages without the log behind them — just the record as it happened.
