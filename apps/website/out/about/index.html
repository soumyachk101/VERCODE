About VERCODE

AI code review has a noise problem. A reviewer that posts a long list of low-confidence comments trains a team to stop reading it — the same failure mode, at the same cost, as any bot a team ignores. VERCODE was built around the opposite property: a review a team actually reads.

The answer is deterministic-first. Static analysis runs before the model, so the findings that matter are grounded in a measured check, not a free-form second opinion. A low confidence finding is suppressed by the floor, not down-ranked into the noise. The model is where it earns its spend: the deep review and the security pass, task-routed to the right tier.

The posture is open source. The pipeline, the static-analysis rules, and the dashboard are in the repository under the MIT license. A number in the docs is a claim you can check against the code; the known limitations are written down, not quietly absent.
