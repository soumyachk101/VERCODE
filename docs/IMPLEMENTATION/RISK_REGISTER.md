# VERCODE — Risk Register

The *active* risk register: the risks that are *open* (a *not-yet-mitigated* or a *mitigation-in-progress*), a *named* owner + a *due date* + a *verification test*. The *threat model* (`THREAT_MODEL.md`) is the *security* risk register (a *the threats*); this is the *product/engineering* risk register (a *the delivery + the operational + the commercial risks*). The *incident response* (`INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`) is the *response* to a *realized* risk (a *this register is the *preventive* list*).

## 1. The *risk* *principle* (a *a risk is a named, owned, verified, tracked unit*)

- **A *risk* has a *named owner*** (a *a risk without an owner is not a risk, it's a worry*; a *`INCIDENT_RESPONSE §8 "a post-incident produces named system fixes that are tracked to closure"`*).
- **A *risk* has a *verification test*** (a *the risk is "mitigated" when the test passes, not when the doc says so*; a *`RELEASE_CRITERIA §2 "the pass is a measurement"`*).
- **A *risk* that's *accepted* is *documented*** (a *a residual risk, a *`THREAT_MODEL §residual risks "accepted, documented"`* — a *an accepted risk is a risk-register entry with a sign-off + a due date for re-evaluation*, a *`INCIDENT_RESPONSE §8 "the actions with a named owner + a due date + the verification test"`*).
- **A *risk* that's *mitigated* is *closed*** (a *the test passes + the docs match*; a *a closed risk is a release-gate pass, a `RELEASE_CRITERIA`*).

## 3. The *risk* *review cadence* (a *a the register is maintained, not a one-time list*)

- **A *risk* is *re-reviewed* on *every release*** (a *the `INCIDENT_RESPONSE §8 "the post-incident produces named system fixes that are tracked to closure"` — a *a SEV-1/SEV-2 post-incident adds a risk-register entry + a system fix*).
- **A *risk* is *re-reviewed* on *every provider/model/prompt change*** (a *the `SECURITY_TEST_PLAN §5 "on a provider/model/prompt change: the full benchmark + the injection suite re-run"` — a *a routing-table change is a R-D4 + a R-C4 re-evaluation*).
- **A *risk* is *re-reviewed* on *every schema change*** (a *the `TENANT_ISOLATION §5 "re-run on every schema change"` — a *a new tenant table that forgot its `org_id` is a R-tenant-isolation re-evaluation*).
- **A *risk* is *closed* when its verification test passes + the docs match + a due date is met** (a *a closed risk is a `RELEASE_CRITERIA` pass; a *a open risk is a named + a owned + a tracked*).

## 4. The *risk* *reporting* (a *the "don't claim what you can't verify" rule applied to risk*)

- **A *risk* that's *accepted* is *documented* (a *a residual risk, a *`THREAT_MODEL §residual "accepted, documented"`* — a *a "we accept the X risk because [the mitigation + the bounded blast radius]" is a statement, a *not a "we think it's fine"*).*
- **A *risk* that's *mitigated* is *closed with a test* (a *a `RELEASE_CRITERIA` pass, a *a "the risk is closed because the ST-* test passes on [date]"*, a *not a "we believe it's mitigated"*).*
- **A *risk* that's *open* is *named + a owned + a tracked to a due date* (a *a "the T-16 allowBuilds placeholder is open; the owner is [the platform engineer]; the due date is [before the first live deploy]; the verification is the CI supply-chain gate"*, a *a not a "we'll get to it"*).*
